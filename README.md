# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

## Why I Love It

*Breaking Bad* is a masterpiece of storytelling, character development, and suspense. It follows the transformation of Walter White from a humble chemistry teacher into a feared drug kingpin.

## Top Reasons to Watch

*Complex Characters* – Every character has depth and evolves over time.
*Intense Plot Twists* – The story keeps you guessing with every episode.
*Cinematic Quality* – The visuals and direction are movie-level.
*Moral Dilemmas* – It makes you question right and wrong.
*Iconic Quotes* – “I am the one who knocks.”

## Main Characters

- Walter White (Bryan Cranston)
- Jesse Pinkman (Aaron Paul)
- Skyler White
- Hank Schrader
- Saul Goodman
